Tempo for macOS & iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Tempo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. All data stays on your devices and in your personal iCloud account.

This policy covers both Tempo for macOS and Tempo for iOS, including home screen widgets. There are no analytics, no tracking, no servers, and no third-party SDKs that receive your information.

Data Stored on Your Devices

Tempo stores the following data locally on your Mac and iPhone using system storage (UserDefaults and App Group containers).

⚙️
Settings & Preferences
Timer durations, session names, colors, themes, icons, sounds, and display options you configure in the app.
📋
Tasks
Task names, subtitles, rich-text notes, tags, due dates, and completion status that you enter in the task list.
📊
Session History & Stats
A local log of completed focus and break sessions, task completion counts, and streak data used to display your statistics. Completed tasks are automatically removed after 60 days.
🎮
Game Stats
Win/loss counts and high scores for the built-in mini-games (2048, Minesweeper, Tic Tac Toe, Memory, Theseus, Nonogram). macOS only.

iCloud Sync

Tempo uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) to sync your tasks, active task selection, completion statistics, and session stats between your Mac and iPhone.

This data is stored in your personal iCloud account, managed entirely by Apple. Tempo does not operate any servers and has no access to your iCloud data outside of the app.

Synced data includes task content (titles, notes, tags, due dates), completion history, and focus session statistics. No personal identifiers are included in the synced data.

You can disable iCloud sync for Tempo in System Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud.

Widgets

Tempo offers home screen widgets on iOS that display your tasks, focus session stats, and completion history. Widget data is stored in a shared App Group container on your device so the widget extension can read it.

No widget data is transmitted externally. The widgets read the same local data that the main app writes.

What We Don't Collect

Tempo never accesses or collects:

🚫
No personal information never
No name, email, Apple ID, or account of any kind is required or collected.
🚫
No usage analytics never
No analytics SDK, crash reporter, or usage tracking is included in the app.
🚫
No external network requests never
Tempo makes no network connections to our servers. The only network activity is iCloud sync handled by Apple's system frameworks.
🚫
No third-party sharing never
No data is shared with advertisers, partners, or any third party.

Notifications

Tempo requests permission to send notifications on both macOS and iOS to alert you when a focus session or break ends, and to remind you of tasks with due dates.

This permission is optional — the app is fully functional without it. All notifications are generated locally on your device. No notification content is transmitted externally.

Accessibility Permission (macOS)

The optional global keyboard shortcut (⌘⇧T) requires macOS Accessibility permission. This permission is used solely to listen for that key combination system-wide. Tempo does not read, record, or transmit any other keyboard input or screen content.

This permission is entirely optional and applies to the macOS app only.

Deleting Your Data

On macOS, you can reset all session and game stats from Settings → Stats → Reset All Stats. You can clear all completed tasks from Settings → Clear Completed Tasks.

On iOS, you can clear completed tasks from Settings → Data → Clear All Completed.

Completed tasks are automatically removed after 60 days on both platforms. Aggregate completion statistics (daily counts) are preserved for up to one year for your historical records.

Uninstalling Tempo removes all locally stored data from your device. To remove synced data from iCloud, disable iCloud sync before uninstalling or manage your iCloud storage in System Settings.

Children's Privacy

Tempo does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app complies with COPPA and applicable children's privacy laws.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Because no personal data is collected, changes are unlikely to affect your privacy in any material way.
